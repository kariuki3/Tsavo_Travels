TSAVO TRAILS EXPERIENCE · TRAIL 01

The Savannah Loop

Enjoy wide-open plains and spectacular wildlife viewing as you explore the beautiful African savannah.

Trail Information

Distance 5 km
Difficulty Moderate
Estimated Time 2–3 hours

WHAT TO EXPECT

Trail Highlights

Open savannah plains, elephants and other wildlife, and excellent photography opportunities.

Keep your camera and binoculars ready as you take in the wide landscapes and watch wildlife in its natural habitat.

Before You Go

Bring drinking water, wear suitable footwear and follow your safari guide's safety instructions. Always observe wildlife from a safe distance.
