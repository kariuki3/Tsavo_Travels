Tsavo Trails | Trail 1

Trail 1: The Savannah Loop

Experience the vast savannahs of Tsavo National Park on this scenic loop trail. Enjoy sightings of elephants, lions, and a variety of bird species as you traverse the open landscapes.

  • Distance: 5 km
  • Difficulty: Moderate
  • Estimated Time: 2-3 hours

Make sure to bring your camera and binoculars for an unforgettable wildlife experience!

Trail Highlights

This trail offers a unique opportunity to witness the diverse wildlife of Tsavo National Park. Keep an eye out for elephants, lions, giraffes, and a variety of bird species as you explore the savannah landscapes.

Don't forget to bring your camera and binoculars for an unforgettable wildlife experience!
