About Us

Sharing the beauty of Kenya's wildlife and natural landscapes.

Tsavo Travels is a safari travel concept created for visitors who want to experience Kenya's wildlife, landscapes and outdoor adventures. We aim to make trip planning clear, welcoming and enjoyable.

Our Mission

Our mission is to connect travellers with memorable nature experiences while encouraging respect for wildlife and the environment.

Our Values

  • Respect for wildlifeWe encourage responsible wildlife viewing.
  • Guest experienceWe value helpful information and friendly service.
  • Care for natureWe promote appreciation of Kenya's natural heritage.
