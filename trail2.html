TSAVO TRAILS EXPERIENCE · TRAIL 02

The River Walk

Discover the plants, birds and animals that thrive around rivers and natural water sources.

Trail Information

Distance 3 km
Difficulty Easy
Estimated Time 1–2 hours

WHAT TO EXPECT

Trail Highlights

River scenery, water birds, plants and wildlife around natural water sources.

Move at a relaxed pace, observe the river environment and look out for birds and animals visiting the water.

Before You Go

Bring drinking water, wear suitable footwear and follow your safari guide's safety instructions. Always observe wildlife from a safe distance.
